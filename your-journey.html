Your Journey

Because it all starts with you

Your Path to Healing, Step by Step

Reaching out for help takes courage. We want to make the rest easy. At Apex Behavioral Health, we believe clarity reduces fear. So here is exactly what you can expect — from your first call to ongoing support. No surprises. No confusion. Just compassionate care, every step of the way.

The 5-Step Journey

1. First Contact

You reach out. We listen.

Call us or email us. We'll match you with the right provider and schedule your first appointment — usually within 1–2 weeks.

  • Warm, judgment-free conversation
  • Clear explanation of services and fees
  • Insurance verification handled for you

2. Intake & Paperwork

We handle the details. You focus on you.

You'll receive a secure link to complete your intake forms — medical history, current symptoms, medications, and goals.

  • HIPAA-compliant patient portal
  • Takes about 20–30 minutes
  • Our team reviews everything before your visit

3. First Appointment (Evaluation)

We get to know you. Deeply.

Your first visit is 60–90 minutes of unhurried, dedicated attention. We listen to your story, understand your struggles, and start building a picture of how we can help.

  • Private, comfortable, judgment-free space
  • In-person or telehealth — you choose
  • Collaborative discussion of diagnosis and treatment options

4. Personalized Treatment Plan

A plan designed just for you.

Your provider works with you to create a customized plan that fits your unique needs and goals. No one-size-fits-all templates. Just care that feels personal.

  • May include therapy, medication, coaching, or lifestyle changes
  • Written summary delivered within 48 hours
  • You're in control — every decision is collaborative

5. Ongoing Support & Follow-Up

We're with you for the long haul.

Healing isn't linear. We provide consistent, ongoing support — whether you need weekly sessions, monthly check-ins, or occasional tune-ups.

  • Regular follow-ups and progress tracking
  • Medication adjustments when needed
  • A consistent provider who knows your history
  • Crisis planning and between-session support

Quick FAQs

Q: Do I need a referral?

A: No. You can reach out to us directly.

Q: What if I don't know what to say?

A: That's normal. We'll guide the conversation.

Q: Can I switch between in-person and telehealth?

A: Absolutely. You choose what works best for you.

Q: What if I need help between appointments?

A: You can reach us through our secure patient portal or by phone.
