Psychiatric Evaluation

What Is a psychiatric evaluation?

A psychiatric evaluation is a comprehensive, in-depth look at your mental, emotional, and behavioral health — the foundation for your entire treatment plan. At Apex, we don't rush it: your first session runs 60–90 minutes so we can get an accurate picture before recommending anything.

How It Works

  • Intake paperwork — a secure link to complete your history before your visit, so we don't waste time on it in person.
  • Clinical interview (60–90 min) — a one-on-one conversation about your symptoms, history, and goals.
  • Mental status exam — a gentle assessment of mood, thought patterns, and focus. Not a test you can fail.
  • Collaborative diagnosis & plan — we explain what we're seeing in plain language and build a plan together.
  • Follow-up scheduled — before you leave, so there's no gap in your care.

What We Evaluate

  • Anxiety and depressive disorders
  • Bipolar disorder and other mood disorders
  • ADHD in adolescents and adults
  • PTSD, OCD, and trauma-related conditions
  • Adjustment, psychotic, and personality disorders

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a referral?

A: No — you can schedule directly with us. We're happy to coordinate with your PCP if you'd like.

Q: Is this covered by insurance?

A: We accept self-pay, Medicare, Medicaid, Cigna, Aetna, United Healthcare, and CareFirst. Contact our office to confirm your specific coverage.
